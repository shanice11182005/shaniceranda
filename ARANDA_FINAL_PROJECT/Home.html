ABOUT US

STAR C CONSTRUCTION SERVICES is a sole proprietorship engaged in general and engineering construction, composed of experienced, industrious, and highly skilled personnel who are capable of efficiently implementing and managing a wide range of contracted projects.

STAR C CONSTRUCTION SERVICES was founded on November 24, 2014. It began with a focus on Architectural Design and later expanded upon recognizing the need to bridge the gap between high-quality, standards-compliant services and affordability, delivering cost-effective solutions without compromising on quality.

VISION

We are engaged in Architectural Design, Architectural and Engineering activities, Interior Fit-Out, Technical Consultancy, Building Construction, Concrete Works, and General and Civil Works. We also specialize in the fabrication of aluminum and glass works, plumbing works, and architectural interiors.
